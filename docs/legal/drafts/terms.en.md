## 1. What Masari is
Masari helps passengers find shared rides and send parcels on routes between Palestinian towns, and helps drivers and shop owners offer those services. Masari connects the people involved. Unless a screen in the app says otherwise, Masari does not own the vehicles, does not employ the drivers and is not a party to the journey itself.

## 4. Passengers
- Fares and seat availability shown in the app are what applies when you book. A booking can be cancelled or changed by the driver or by Masari if the trip cannot run safely or lawfully.
- Be at the pick-up point on time. Repeated no-shows may limit your account.
- Follow the driver's reasonable safety instructions, including using seat belts.
- Do not carry anything unlawful or dangerous.

## 5. Drivers
- You must hold a valid driving licence and vehicle registration for the vehicle you use, and the insurance the law requires. You must upload the documents we ask for. We review them before you can offer trips, and you must renew them before they expire.
- You decide whether to accept a trip, but once you accept one you must make a reasonable effort to complete it safely and on time.
- Drive lawfully, do not carry more passengers than the vehicle has seats for, and do not use the app while driving.
- A driver trust score based on trip reliability and passenger feedback may affect the trips offered to you.

## 6. Shop owners and parcels
- Shop owners and passengers who send parcels must describe the contents truthfully and must not send prohibited, dangerous or unlawful items.
- A parcel is handed over to the named receiver, who may be asked for a code. Do not share that code with anyone else.
- Compensation for lost or damaged parcels, if any, is limited to what the app states at the time of sending and what the law requires.
