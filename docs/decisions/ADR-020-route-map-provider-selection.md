# ADR-020: route/map provider selection

Status: proposed; M7D1 closed; M7D1B active and blocked on remaining evidence; final provider not selected.

Masari needs immutable canonical route geometry but must not couple matching or Trip creation to provider SDKs. Mapbox began as the conditional hosted bakeoff lead. Google, HERE, Stadia-hosted geocoding/routing, and the separate `OSM_SELF_HOSTED_VALHALLA` candidate are evaluated. MapLibre itself is a renderer, not a geocoder or router. Routing, geocoding, tiles and rendering may be selected separately.

Decision for M7D1: adopt the provider-neutral server abstraction, deterministic fake, protected entity-referenced preview, fixed-host adapters, fail-closed configuration, no fallback, and deterministic checksum. Do not persist preview results or enable any production provider. Do not add a client renderer SDK.

M7D1B evidence outcome: Mapbox, HERE, and Stadia remain `NOT_EXECUTED`. Independent review corrected materially misplaced PPU and Bab Al-Zawiya fixtures. The checksum-pinned Valhalla 3.8.3 rerun passed corrected 4/4 required routes, 12/12 expanded structural routes and selected restriction controls; warm/process-cold/mixed-20 p95 values were `14.627/66.984/25.110 ms`. Real-world access remains conditional. On the unchanged 60-query corpus, Nominatim scored 51/60, Pelias 25/60, Photon 45/60, and official Google address Geocoding API v4 21/60 after functional review (Arabic 11/30; English 10/30); every geocoder fails all 95% quality gates. A separate Google Places Text Search run scored Arabic 25/30, English 23/30 and overall top-1 48/60; top-5 was 52/60. Places is better suited to named-place discovery and materially outperformed address geocoding on this corpus, but still fails every 95% gate and its content storage remains restricted. Nominatim remains both measured quality leaders at 85.0% and is still unapproved.

The corrected Google Routes audit used documented `location.latLng` nesting, a minimal mask, normal stopovers, and a secondary Place-ID control. All requests returned HTTP 200 with empty response objects and zero routes. The controls do not prove a construction, field-mask, or snapping defect and cannot distinguish a Demo-Key/account/backend limitation from another unexplained condition despite official Palestine driving coverage. Therefore `GOOGLE_ROUTING_EVIDENCE=INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`; Valhalla still returned all four reviewed routes. Google Places storage is `RESTRICTED`, while Place ID storage is `PERMITTED_WITH_REFRESH_POLICY`; neither approves production persistence. Systematic OSM canonical route geometry remains `UNRESOLVED / LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`; renderer attribution and production operations remain conditional. Therefore `VALHALLA_ROUTING_CANDIDATE=CONDITIONAL`, `ROUTING_RECOMMENDATION_CANDIDATE=OSM_VALHALLA`, `GEOCODING_QUALITY_LEADER=NOMINATIM`, `SEARCH_QUALITY_LEADER=NOMINATIM`, `GEOCODING_RECOMMENDATION_CANDIDATE=NONE`, and `PROVIDER_SELECTION=NO_PROVIDER_APPROVED_YET`.

Consequences: M7D1B remains `ACTIVE / BLOCKED_ON_REMAINING_PROVIDER_EVIDENCE`. M7D2 cannot begin until independent review approves complete quality, storage, attribution, privacy, commercial and operational evidence for a chosen routing/geocoding/renderer combination, or explicitly accepts a no-provider outcome and lists the next evidence. M7E remains prohibited. Any provider or component switch is explicit because provenance, geometry behavior, storage rights, attribution, telemetry and cost differ. Focused self-hosted evidence is recorded in `osm-valhalla-palestine-evidence.md`, `osm-self-hosted-operational-evaluation.md`, and `osm-nominatim-evidence.md` alongside the existing provider documents.
