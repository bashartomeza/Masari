# ADR-020: route/map provider selection

Status: proposed; M7D1 closed; M7D1B active and blocked on remaining evidence; final provider not selected.

Masari needs immutable canonical route geometry but must not couple matching or Trip creation to provider SDKs. Mapbox began as the conditional hosted bakeoff lead. Google, HERE, Stadia-hosted geocoding/routing, and the separate `OSM_SELF_HOSTED_VALHALLA` candidate are evaluated. MapLibre itself is a renderer, not a geocoder or router. Routing, geocoding, tiles and rendering may be selected separately.

Decision for M7D1: adopt the provider-neutral server abstraction, deterministic fake, protected entity-referenced preview, fixed-host adapters, fail-closed configuration, no fallback, and deterministic checksum. Do not persist preview results or enable any production provider. Do not add a client renderer SDK.

M7D1B evidence outcome: Mapbox, HERE, and Stadia remain `NOT_EXECUTED`. Independent review corrected materially misplaced PPU and Bab Al-Zawiya fixtures. The checksum-pinned Valhalla 3.8.3 rerun passed corrected 4/4 required routes, 12/12 expanded structural routes and selected restriction controls; warm/process-cold/mixed-20 p95 values were `14.627/66.984/25.110 ms`. Real-world access remains conditional. On the unchanged 60-query corpus, Nominatim scored 51/60, Pelias 25/60, Photon 45/60, and official Google Geocoding API v4 21/60 after functional review (Arabic 11/30; English 10/30); every geocoder fails all 95% quality gates. Google's bounded 60-request p50/p95 was `111.201/128.934 ms`, but quality—not transport—disqualifies it. Four optional official Google Routes API calls returned HTTP success with zero route objects, while Valhalla returned all four reviewed routes. Google production storage is independently `RESTRICTED`; systematic OSM canonical route geometry remains `UNRESOLVED / LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`; renderer attribution and production operations remain conditional. Therefore `VALHALLA_ROUTING_CANDIDATE=CONDITIONAL`, `ROUTING_RECOMMENDATION_CANDIDATE=OSM_VALHALLA`, every tested geocoder is `FAIL`, `GEOCODING_RECOMMENDATION_CANDIDATE=NONE`, and `PROVIDER_SELECTION=NO_PROVIDER_APPROVED_YET`.

Consequences: M7D1B remains `ACTIVE / BLOCKED_ON_REMAINING_PROVIDER_EVIDENCE`. M7D2 cannot begin until independent review approves complete quality, storage, attribution, privacy, commercial and operational evidence for a chosen routing/geocoding/renderer combination, or explicitly accepts a no-provider outcome and lists the next evidence. M7E remains prohibited. Any provider or component switch is explicit because provenance, geometry behavior, storage rights, attribution, telemetry and cost differ. Focused self-hosted evidence is recorded in `osm-valhalla-palestine-evidence.md`, `osm-self-hosted-operational-evaluation.md`, and `osm-nominatim-evidence.md` alongside the existing provider documents.
